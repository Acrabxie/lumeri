Last updated 19 August 2026

Prohibited generation

These six categories may not be generated on this platform. They are listed individually rather than as a general clause so there is no ambiguity about what is covered.

以下六类内容不得在本平台生成。逐条列出而非笼统概括，是为了不留解释空间。

  1. Sexual content

    Explicit sexual activity, or nudity presented pornographically.

    露骨性行为，或以色情方式呈现的裸体。

    Not covered: artistic nudity that is not pornographic. 不属于此类：非色情的艺术裸体。

  2. Graphic violence

    Gore, or realistic severe injury depicted for its own sake.

    血腥内容，或为呈现本身而描绘的写实重伤。

    Not covered: war, history, illness, injustice and other serious subjects treated seriously; stylised or game violence. 不属于此类：严肃对待的战争、历史、疾病、不公等题材，以及风格化或游戏化的暴力。

  3. Hate speech

    Content attacking or demeaning people on the basis of a protected characteristic.

    基于受保护特征攻击或贬低他人的内容。

    Not covered: depicting historical hatred in order to document or criticise it. 不属于此类：为记录或批判而呈现历史上的仇恨。

  4. Child-unsafe content

    Any sexualised depiction of a minor, and any other content that endangers children. This category is not weighed against creative intent, and an uncertain judgement resolves against generation.

    任何对未成年人的性化描绘，以及其他危害儿童的内容。此类不与创作意图作权衡，判断存疑时一律拒绝。

  5. Deepfakes and impersonation

    Realistic depictions of a real person presented as genuine without their consent, and deceptive impersonation of a person or organisation.

    未经本人同意、以假乱真地呈现真实人物，以及对个人或机构的欺骗性冒充。

    Not covered: clearly labelled satire and commentary. 不属于此类：明确标示的讽刺与评论。

  6. Copyright and trademark infringement

    Requests to reproduce a protected work, a brand mark, or a character in a way that infringes the rights holder.

    要求复制受保护作品、品牌标识或角色，且构成对权利人侵权的行为。

    Note: this is enforced on what you ask for, not by inspecting rendered frames for brand marks. If your rights have been infringed, use the reporting address below. 说明：此项针对请求内容执行，不通过检测画面中的品牌标识实现。若您的权利受到侵害，请通过下方地址举报。

How this is enforced

Enforcement happens on the platform itself, not only in the models it calls.

审核由平台自身执行，而非仅依赖所调用的模型。

  • Before generation

    Every generation request is screened before it reaches any model.

    每个生成请求在送达模型之前都会被审核。

  • After generation, before display

    Generated images and video are screened again before the result is returned. A refused result is not shown and is not kept.

    生成的图像与视频在返回前会再次审核。被拒绝的结果不会展示，也不会留存。

  • Switching models does not bypass it

    Screening runs at the point where any tool is invoked, so it applies to every model this platform can reach — including models supplied by the user.

    审核发生在工具调用处，因此适用于平台可访问的每一个模型，包括用户自行接入的模型。

  • Refusals are recorded

    Each refusal is logged with its time, category and a fingerprint. The refused text or media is never stored — storing it would mean keeping exactly the material that was just refused.

    每次拒绝都会记录时间、类别与指纹。被拒绝的文本或媒体本身绝不留存——留存等同于保留刚被拒绝的内容。

What happens on a violation

A single refused request

The request is declined and nothing is generated. No further action is taken — a refusal is not a strike.

请求被拒绝，不生成任何内容。不作进一步处理——一次拒绝不构成违规记录。

Repeated or deliberate attempts

Repeatedly reformulating a refused request to get around screening may lead to rate limiting, suspension of generation features, or account termination.

反复改写被拒请求以规避审核，可能导致速率限制、暂停生成功能或终止账户。

Child-unsafe content

Handled separately from every other category: immediate termination, and reporting to the competent authorities where the law requires it.

与其他类别分开处理：立即终止账户，并在法律要求时向主管机关报告。

Reporting

To report content generated with Lumeri, an infringement of your rights, or a failure of the screening described above, write to safety@lumeri.io.

如需举报使用 Lumeri 生成的内容、侵犯您权利的行为，或上述审核机制的失效，请发送邮件至 safety@lumeri.io。

Please include what you saw and, where possible, how it was produced. Reports about children are handled ahead of everything else.
